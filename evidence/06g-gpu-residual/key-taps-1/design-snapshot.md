The preceding output-only interval replay reproduced the sealed 06f group 0 endpoints and states under both policies. In the warm chunk, layer 2 input and pre-attention normalized output were byte-exact, but the raw RoPE key sum `x_219` differed at one element and the difference survived in `k_51` and the layer 2 key cache. Shapes have dynamic query length 1–32 with fixed remaining dimensions. No score or attention-weight tensor is exposed.

The source is the sealed 06f one-site-corrected group 0 package.
