There is a qualification on the target rationale. The 06f replay shows warm `inner_k_cache_1` is exact across CPU and CPU_AND_GPU, while `inner_k_cache_2` is the first differing cache. Since `var_7154` feeds layer-1 normalization before the layer-1 K/V projections, an exact tap chain through `x_93` would make this site a useful negative finding rather than a likely explanation of the cache-2 mismatch. Inventory order alone does not connect it causally to that later mismatch.

If the warm `var_7154` chain is exact, the next narrow localization should bracket the interval after the layer-1 cache writes and before the layer-2 K-cache write: first expose layer-1 attention/softmax output and post-attention residual/norm, then the MLP/residual output or layer-2 pre-attention normalization input, alongside the earliest layer-2 K projection/update boundary. Prefer the smallest tap set that finds the earliest CPU/GPU-policy difference; the observed cache boundary does not by itself identify which intervening operation diverges. Keep same-policy original-versus-tapped endpoints and all-state controls, and treat a tap mismatch as localization evidence rather than proof of a repair.

## Key replay-1 independent raw audit

All source/protocol pins and input archives verified. I independently rehashed and checked every output/state archive and recomputed the four same-policy controls: original outputs and all eight states match saved 06f arrays exactly, and tapped endpoints/states match each original exactly. The warm projection taps `x_197` and `value_41` and the key norm denominator `denom_21` are exact across policies. Its reciprocal seed `var_14324` differs at 9 of 24 values by one ULP; one value moves a quotient across a half-integer (`-169.5` on CPU versus `-169.49998474` on CPU_AND_GPU), and both policy results follow ties-to-even for their own quotients. The reciprocal denominator range is 24.06–58.72, outside the previously tested helper domain `[0.5,8]`, so this evidence does not support applying that helper here.

The only warm final RoPE-sum difference is at `[0,0,0,73]`: CPU `x_219` is `-0.33203125`, CPU_AND_GPU is `-0.33007812`. At that index the cosine product `var_14415` has those same respective values, while the sine product `var_14438` is `+0.0` under both policies. Thus this surviving difference is carried by the cosine arm at that element. Separately, 775 `var_14438` elements differ only in signed-zero bits despite zero numeric difference; that is not the source of the nonzero `x_219` delta at the reported index. The final round and `k_51` preserve one differing element. This locates the mismatch in the key-normalization/quantization trajectory and shows it surviving through the cosine RoPE arm; it does not prove which backend primitive caused the reciprocal-seed discrepancy or establish a repair. The separate CPU-seed substitution experiment should remain an isolated diagnostic with its own controls.
